Building the Next Generation of Condominium Leadership.

Condominium boards are running multi-million-dollar organizations with volunteer leadership and surprisingly little operational support. We're working to change that.

Project Baseplate is a community of condominium board leaders and advisors working to improve governance, operations, and resident experience through peer collaboration, proven frameworks, and practical expertise. We are building the methodologies, operating practices, and professional networks needed to modernize how condominium communities are governed and managed.

PB–01

Why We Exist

Condominium associations are responsible for complex organizations that include:

  • Multi-million-dollar operating budgets
  • Significant reserve funds and capital projects
  • Professional staff and vendors
  • Complex financial, legal, operational, and governance requirements
  • The homes and investments of hundreds of residents

Yet most boards are composed of volunteers.

Across Chicago, board leaders encounter many of the same challenges:

  • Management companies focused on administration rather than leadership
  • Inconsistent policies, procedures, and operating practices
  • Limited access to strategic planning and organizational expertise
  • Poor communication, transparency, and accountability
  • Institutional knowledge that disappears as board leadership changes
  • Major projects executed without proven frameworks or methodologies
  • Volunteer board members pulled into work that should be handled by management

The result is a recurring cycle of reactive decision-making, operational inefficiency, and frustration for boards, residents, staff, and management teams.

We believe condominium communities deserve better.

PB–02

What Is Project Baseplate?

Project Baseplate is an initiative focused on raising the standard of condominium governance and operations.

We bring together engaged board leaders, experienced operators, and subject-matter experts to share knowledge, develop practical frameworks, and build the operational foundation that condominium communities need to thrive.

We are not another management company.

Project Baseplate serves as the umbrella for a growing community and an emerging set of advisory services, frameworks, and practical tools.

We are building the expertise, methodologies, and community that boards often expect from management companies but rarely receive.

Project Baseplate Collective

Learn from leaders facing the same challenges.

Project Baseplate Collective is a growing network of condominium board presidents, officers, committee leaders, and former board members committed to improving how their communities operate.

Together, members share:

  • Lessons learned from serving on condominium boards
  • Real-world operating practices
  • Capital project experiences
  • Governance approaches
  • Policy and procedure frameworks
  • Management company insights
  • Practical solutions to recurring challenges

The Collective creates a trusted forum for candid conversation among people who understand the responsibilities, constraints, and opportunities facing condominium boards.

No board should have to solve every problem alone.

Project Baseplate Advisory

Practical expertise for boards pursuing operational excellence.

Project Baseplate Advisory helps condominium associations address consequential organizational and operational challenges that frequently fall outside the capabilities of traditional management arrangements.

Potential areas of focus include:

  • Board governance and effectiveness
  • Operational assessments
  • Strategic and long-range planning
  • Management company evaluations and transitions
  • Staffing and organizational design
  • Policy and procedure development
  • Resident communication and transparency
  • Capital project governance
  • Performance measurement and accountability
  • Documentation of repeatable management systems

Our purpose is straightforward:

Help condominium communities run with the discipline of a well-managed organization and the transparency their residents deserve.

Project Baseplate Frameworks

Practical systems for the work boards should not have to invent from scratch.

Project Baseplate is developing reusable methodologies, templates, and operating practices that condominium boards and their management partners can adapt to the needs of their communities.

The long-term framework library may include:

  • Board operating frameworks
  • Annual governance and planning calendars
  • Management transition playbooks
  • Capital project governance models
  • Staffing and organizational templates
  • Resident communication standards
  • Operational policies and procedures
  • Board packet and management-reporting standards
  • Strategic planning methodologies
  • Contract and vendor-management systems

These are the foundational tools boards often assume their management companies already provide, but too often do not.

PB–06

Who We Serve

Project Baseplate is initially focused on Chicago high-rise condominium communities, particularly those with:

We are especially interested in connecting with current and former board leaders who believe condominium governance can and should be better.

  • Approximately 100 to 500 residential units
  • Volunteer-led boards
  • Professional management
  • On-site building staff and complex daily operations
  • Significant capital, staffing, vendor, and governance responsibilities
  • A desire for greater operational maturity, transparency, and accountability
PB–07

Why “Baseplate”?

A baseplate is the foundation that helps a structure stand securely, carry weight, and operate as designed.

Too many condominium communities operate without the foundational systems, processes, institutional knowledge, and leadership frameworks required for long-term success.

Project Baseplate exists to help build that foundation.
